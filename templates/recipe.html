🍛 Tomato Egg Fried Rice

⏱ Preparation Time: 20 Minutes

🔥 Calories: 420 kcal

🥗 Difficulty: Easy

👨‍🍳 Servings: 2 People

🥕 Ingredients

  • 1 Cup Cooked Rice
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 Tomato
  • 1 Onion
  • 2 tsp Oil
  • Salt to Taste
  • Black Pepper
  • Fresh Coriander

👨‍🍳 Cooking Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add chopped onions and sauté until golden.
  3. Add tomatoes and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Add beaten eggs and scramble.
  5. Add cooked rice.
  6. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Mix everything well.
  8. Garnish with coriander and serve hot.

💡 AI Health Tip

Add peas and carrots to increase fiber and vitamins. Serve with cucumber salad for a balanced meal.
